How to use SparkLab

  1. Add parts. Click anything in the Parts drawer on the left and it appears on the bench.
  2. Move them. Drag a part around to tidy up your layout.
  3. Wire them up. Every part has a small circle on each side — these are its legs. Click one circle, then click another, and a wire connects them.
  4. Make a loop. Electricity only flows in a complete circle: out of the battery's +, through your parts, and back into the battery's −. No loop, nothing happens.
  5. Mind the LED. An LED only works one way round. Its + leg must face the battery's +.
  6. Always use a resistor. An LED wired straight to a battery gets far too much current and would burn out in real life. SparkLab warns you instead of letting it die.

Delete a part with the × that appears when you hover over it. Delete a wire by clicking it.
